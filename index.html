TRADE WITH CARE

Make informed marketplace decisions.

Product information and seller details help you make decisions, but you should independently verify important transaction details.

✓

Verify product details

Confirm product availability, quantity, condition and price with the seller.

✓

Confirm seller information

Review available storefront details and ask appropriate questions before proceeding.

✓

Protect your account

Never share your password, verification codes or sensitive financial credentials.

✓

Understand payment status

Do not treat an order request as proof of payment. Online payments require a verified payment-provider integration.
